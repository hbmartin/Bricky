# Bricky — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** April 2026

Bricky ("the App", "we", "us") is a LEGO® companion app for iPhone and iPad. We
built Bricky to be private by design: nearly everything happens on your device,
we do not require an account, and we do not sell or share your personal data.
This policy explains what is and isn't collected, and the choices you have.

## 1. Summary

- No account or sign-up is required to use the App.
- Brick and minifigure scanning runs entirely on-device with Core ML. Camera
  images are processed locally and are not uploaded to us.
- We do not operate servers that store your personal data.
- No advertising, no ad tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.
- Optional iCloud Sync stores your data in **your** personal Apple iCloud
  account, not ours.
- Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

## 2. Data We Collect

Bricky collects minimal data:

- **Camera images** — Processed on-device for brick and minifigure scanning.
  Images are not transmitted to us and are not retained except as scan history
  you choose to keep on your device.
- **Photo library images** — Only the images you explicitly choose to import are
  read, and only on-device.
- **Location (optional)** — If you enable scan geo-tagging, approximate
  coordinates are attached to a scan so you can remember where pieces were found.
  Coordinates are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.
- **Anonymous analytics (opt-in)** — If you opt in, the App records anonymous
  usage events (e.g., a scan started, a build viewed) to improve features. No
  names, emails, contacts, or precise location are included.
- **Subscription / purchase status** — Managed by Apple via StoreKit. We receive
  only an entitlement (Pro or free); Apple processes all payment information.
- **iCloud content (optional)** — If you enable iCloud Sync, inventories,
  settings, and community content are stored in your personal iCloud account.

## 3. On-Device AI

All brick and minifigure identification uses on-device Core ML models and
pre-computed embeddings bundled with the App. Scanning works fully offline and
your images never leave the device for core identification.

## 4. Community Features

If you use community features, content you choose to publish (e.g., posts,
shared builds) is stored in Apple CloudKit under your iCloud identity. Only
content you explicitly publish is shared. You can delete your community content
at any time.

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Bricky is intended to be safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13. The App requires no account, has no
advertising or ad tracking, and shares no data with third parties for marketing.

## 6. Data Storage

Inventories, scan history, and favorites are stored locally on your device. With
iCloud Sync enabled, that data is additionally stored in your personal Apple
iCloud account. We do not operate servers that store your personal data.

## 7. Third-Party Services

- **Apple StoreKit** — In-app purchase processing.
- **Apple iCloud / CloudKit** — Optional, user-controlled sync and community.

No advertising networks, social-media SDKs, or third-party analytics providers
are integrated.

## 8. Your Choices

- Opt out of analytics at any time in **Settings**.
- Delete scan history in **Settings**.
- Disable iCloud Sync at any time in **Settings**.
- Remove all local data by deleting the App; iCloud data can be managed in iOS
  Settings → iCloud.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be reflected
by the "Last updated" date above.
