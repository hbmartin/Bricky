Support

Need help, have a feature request, or found a bug? Send us a message and we'll get back to you.

Does Bricky work offline?

Yes — scanning, identification, inventory, and build suggestions all run on-device with no connection required.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases — Bricky Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. With iCloud Sync enabled, it's in your personal iCloud account, never our servers.
