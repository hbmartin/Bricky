Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Bricky ("the App", "we", "us") is a LEGO® companion app for iPhone and iPad. We built Bricky to be private by design: nearly everything happens on your device, we do not require an account, and we do not sell or share your personal data.

1. Summary

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • Scanning runs on-device with Core ML; camera images are not uploaded to us.
  • No advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Optional iCloud Sync stores data in your personal iCloud account, not ours.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see payment details.

2. Data We Collect

  • Camera images — processed on-device for brick/minifigure scanning; not transmitted to us.
  • Photo library images — only the ones you choose to import, read on-device.
  • Location (optional) — if enabled, approximate coordinates tag a scan; stored only on your device.
  • Anonymous analytics (opt-in) — usage events to improve the app; no personal info.
  • Subscription status — managed by Apple via StoreKit.
  • iCloud content (optional) — inventories/settings in your personal iCloud.

3. On-Device AI

Brick and minifigure identification uses on-device Core ML and bundled embeddings, working fully offline. Your images never leave the device for core identification.

4. Community Features

Content you publish is stored in Apple CloudKit under your iCloud identity. Only content you explicitly publish is shared, and you can delete it anytime.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Bricky is safe for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal info from children under 13. No account, no ads, no third-party data sharing.

6. Data Storage

Inventories, scan history, and favorites are stored on your device. With iCloud Sync, data is also in your personal iCloud account. We don't operate servers that store your personal data.

7. Third-Party Services

Apple StoreKit (purchases) and Apple iCloud/CloudKit (optional sync). No ad networks, social SDKs, or third-party analytics.

8. Your Choices

Opt out of analytics, delete scan history, or disable iCloud Sync in Settings. Deleting the App removes local data.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: use the support form on the home page.
